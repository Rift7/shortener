# Code of Conduct

## Scope

This applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other project space.

## Expectations

- Be direct about code and kind about people. Critique the change, not the author.
- Assume competence: ask what someone was trying to do before assuming they were careless.
- Keep threads on topic. Take unrelated disagreements elsewhere.
- Respect a maintainer's decision to close a discussion that has run its course.

## Not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language, publishing someone's
private information, or sustained disruption of discussions. Security issues
reported publicly instead of through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) will be removed.

## Enforcement

Report concerns to the maintainer at the address on the GitHub profile. Reports
are handled privately. Responses range from a private warning to a permanent ban,
proportionate to the behavior and its pattern.

Maintainers may remove comments, close threads, or block accounts to enforce this.
